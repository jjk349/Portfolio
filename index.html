About me

Hi, I'm James Kurtis, a Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell University. I'm a member of Cornell Racing FSAE's Accumulator (high-voltage battery) sub-team, where over the past year I've helped redesign and manufacture our car's accumulator container — pioneering the use of composite materials for the part — and designed the ARG25 low-voltage box using CAD and 3D-printed prototypes. Working hands-on at the intersection of mechanical and electrical engineering has given me a particular interest in electric vehicles.
