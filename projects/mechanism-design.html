Lifting Mechanism Design

Statics & Mechanics of Solids — Class Project

For my Statics and Mechanics of Solids class, we were tasked with designing a mechanism that maximizes the height and weight a single linear actuator could lift within a fixed design envelope. My design uses a single straight bar pinned at the corner of the allowed space, with the actuator pinned to the ground and its push arm pinned to the middle of the bar — using the bar as a lever arm to balance lift height against lift weight.
