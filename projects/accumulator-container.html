Accumulator Container

Cornell Racing FSAE — Accumulator Sub-team · Oct 2024–Present

Cornell Racing's FSAE car stores its energy in a high-voltage accumulator that has to meet strict FSAE safety rules for containing battery cells while staying as light as possible. I'm redesigning and manufacturing this accumulator container, pioneering the use of composite materials for the part to cut weight without compromising the structure that protects the cells.

Highlights

  • Redesigning and manufacturing the accumulator container from the ground up
  • Pioneering composite materials use for this part on the team
  • Working at the intersection of mechanical design and electrical safety requirements
