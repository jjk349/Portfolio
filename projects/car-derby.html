3D Printed Car Derby

Xavier High School — 1st Place Finisher · Jan–Jun 2024

Our class-wide derby challenged students to design and 3D print a car body optimized for speed and structural integrity. I modeled an aerodynamic car body in Rhino 7, validating the design through iterative refinement, and built each individual car independently while sharing design ideas with peers.

Highlights

  • Modeled an aerodynamic car body in Rhino 7
  • Won 1st place in each of 3 competitive race events
  • Collaborated with peers on design ideas while building cars independently
