ARG25 LV Box

Cornell Racing FSAE — Accumulator Sub-team

The ARG25 low-voltage (LV) box houses the low-voltage electrical components that control and monitor the car's high-voltage system. I designed the enclosure in CAD and used 3D-printed prototypes to check fit and packaging before manufacturing the final components for on-car integration.

Highlights

  • Designed the enclosure in CAD to fit the required electrical components
  • 3D printed prototypes to validate fit and packaging before final manufacturing
  • Manufactured final parts and integrated them onto the car
